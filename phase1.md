1. Objective
The goal of this phase is to move from a broad project idea to a structured, documented set of 
requirements. You will engage in active research to understand your stakeholders and define the scope of 
your system.
2. Requirement Gathering (Discovery)
Each team must employ at least three (3) different methods to gather requirements. You must provide 
documented proof for each method used.
● Method 1: Semi-Structured Interview (Mandatory)
○ Action: Conduct an interview with a potential user or stakeholder ranging from 30 min to 
1hour.
○ Setup: Use professional attire and a clean background; cameras must remain on for the 
duration.
○ Proof Required: A link to the online recording (e.g., Google Drive) and a clean, readable 
transcription of the session.
● Method 2 & 3: Elective Techniques (Choose Two)
○ Ethnography/Observation: Observe how users currently handle the problem you are solving 
(Proof: Observation notes).
○ Surveys/Questionnaires: Collect quantitative data from a wider audience (Proof: Survey link 
and summary of results).
○ Document Analysis: Analyze existing systems or competitor workflows (Proof: Comparative 
analysis report).
○ Focus Groups: Facilitate a group discussion to brainstorm features (Proof: Group meeting 
minutes).
3. Requirement Elicitation & Backlog Management
Once the data is gathered, you must translate these "needs" into actionable "tasks" using JIRA.
● User Stories: Extract functional requirements in the form of User Stories.
● Quantification: You must define at least 3 different user roles with a minimum of 5 user stories 
per role.
● Acceptance Criteria: Every user story in JIRA must include specific acceptance criteria 
(Definition of Done).
● Prioritization: Apply the MoSCoW technique (Must have, Should have, Could have, Won't have) 
to your backlog items.
4. SRS & Documentation (Any real-time project collaboration tool for
documentation)
You are required to initiate your Software Requirements Specification (SRS) document using any tool. 
This document should include:
● System Scope: A clear purpose and problem statement.
● Stakeholder Analysis: A detailed list explaining the roles and interests of all stakeholders.
● Functional & Non-Functional Requirements: Include your prioritized user stories and system 
constraints (Performance, Security, Usability).
● UML Modeling: Initial Use Case Diagrams, Sequence diagrams, Class Diagrams and any other 
relevant context models specialized models to visualize the system architecture.
● Sample structure is given in slides.
5. Submission Requirements
By the deadline, your team must submit a single PDF containing:
1. Requirement Proofs: Links to the interview recording, the transcription, and evidence for your 
two elective methods.
2. JIRA Evidence: 3–4 screenshots of your JIRA board showing user stories, acceptance criteria, and 
prioritization.
3. SRS Draft: A PDF export of your progress on the SRS document from your online collaboration 
tool.
4. Meeting Minutes: A consolidated log of all team syncs and stakeholder meetings held during this 
phase.
