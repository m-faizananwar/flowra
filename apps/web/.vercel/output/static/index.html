How Flowra
works.

Three simple steps to eliminate the overhead of manual board updates and execute project synchronization in real-time.

01
Step One

Connect & Listen

Link Jira, GitHub, and your team chat (Slack, Discord, or Telegram). Flowra's AI agents run silently in the background, listening to every commit, PR, and standup message.

02
Step Two

Verify with Proof

When a developer says "Done with the API," Flowra checks GitHub for the actual commits and PRs — not just their word for it. Real proof of work, automatically.

03
Step Three

Approve & Sync

Flowra suggests a card move on the dashboard. Your PM approves it with one click. Jira updates instantly, staying 100% accurate — zero overhead.
